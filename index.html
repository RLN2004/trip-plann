Before you go

Three rules to protect the plan.

◷
Rest every ~250 km

Take a full 20-minute stop on long legs. Meal, fuel and temple stops are extra; don't push through fatigue.

✓
Recheck temple hours

Check official pages and call if needed the day before, especially Pazhamudircholai and any festival-related changes.

↗
Use live route estimates

Distances, travel times and arrival targets are approximate. Traffic, queues, parking and hill transport can change the day.

!

Honest reality check: Starting at 1:00 PM Friday improves the first day's schedule, but all six temples by midnight Sunday is still a demanding trip with long drives and little schedule slack. Share driving if possible. With one driver, consider adding another day or dropping a temple. Timings, queues and return arrival cannot be guaranteed. Never speed or drive drowsy.
